# fun activity

An Android app that uploads your call recordings to a **Call Recordings** folder in your own Google
Drive.

## What it does

- **Automatic uploads.** Pick one or more folders where your phone saves recordings. New audio
  files there upload automatically, even when the app is closed.
- **Share to upload.** Some phone apps keep recordings private. Share a recording from them and pick
  **Upload to Drive**.
- **No duplicates.** Each recording uploads once, even if it is renamed or shared again.
- **Reliable.** Uploads wait for Wi-Fi (optional), retry on errors, and continue after a restart.
- **WhatsApp audio.** Voice notes and audio files can be added as a folder too.

Supported formats: m4a, mp3, wav, amr, 3gp, aac, ogg, opus.

## Privacy

- The app does not record calls. It only uploads files your phone already saved.
- It can only see the folders you pick and the files it creates in Drive.
- No server, no ads, no analytics.

## How to use

1. Sign in with your Google account.
2. Pick the folder where your recordings are saved.
3. That's it. Check the home screen for upload status, or tap **Sync now**.

Laws on recording calls vary by place, and many require the other person's consent.
